Dagadusheth Halwai Ganapati Temple

The Dagadusheth Halwai Ganapati Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to the Hindu god Ganesha (Ganapati) in the city of Pune, India.The temple is visited by over one hundred thousand devotees every year.[1] A large number of devotees visit the temple during the annual ten-day public Ganeshotsav festival.[2] The main Ganesha idol is insured for a sum of ₹10 million (US$100,000).[3] It celebrated 134 years of its Ganeshotsav festival in 2026.[4]


History

Dagdusheth Halwai was originally from the Gokak town in Karnataka[5]. He later moved to Pune, where he became a successful sweet seller (halwai) and a wealthy businessman in the city during the late 1800s. His original halwai shop still exists under the name “Dagdusheth Halwai Sweets” near Datta Mandir in Pune.
